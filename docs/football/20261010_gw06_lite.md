# Premier League GW06 (10–12 Oct 2026) — football LITE reads, written before kick-off

Eddie, 10 Oct: "just build a 'lite' version today to give a rough idea". Each match is read like a race:
- **The race** is the match, from kick-off to KO+120 (the 105 minutes plus up to 15 added).
- **Pairs.** A "pair" is a player with his own manager. P01 is the home manager and P13 the away manager.

Same methods as the races: M3, the M2 layers (Nodes, L2, L3, L4), same body, and numbers.

**What "lite" changes**
- **Sky.** Computed every minute.
- **Same-body stage.** Scanned every 5 min, then refined to 15 s around the best moment.
- **Method 1 stage not run.** The M3 natal string is the M3 natal chord.
- **Natal charts.** Built here with the engine at 12:00 standard time and the country centroid (the chart-builder rule). TNOs come from Eddie's team workbooks (12:00 UTC).
- **Sky TNOs.** By rate.

The race scorer saturates over 2 hours (Arsenal–Leeds: every pair scored 8.8–10). So each match is laid out by time with `tools/match_lite.py`, giving per match:
- periods;
- Sun–Mars across a pair;
- very tight receivers;
- the managers;
- patterns: a natal body struck in sequence, double same-body triangles, the node axis.

Every read was checked by an independent agent against the table. One count was corrected: Timber had 8 very tight hits, not 9. One "most" claim was dropped: Onyeka, not Lampard, has the most tight hits at Coventry.

These are observations, not rules, and untested on football.
