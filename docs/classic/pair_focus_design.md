## The measure (added to Layer 3, compared in Layer 4, written in Layer 5)
For every pair of bodies {A, B} (Rahu/Ketu folded as Node; A = B allowed, e.g. horse Jupiter – Best Jupiter), list the KINDS of root item
that land on that pair, across horse, jockey and sky:
  shared      the pair's horse and jockey values are shared (within 0.5%)
  cross       a close cross link horse A – jockey B or horse B – jockey A
  struck      that cross link struck on both ends (shape 1)
  partner     sky A lands on the B end or sky B on the A end (shape 2)
  moved       one or both ends of that cross link have moved (shapes 4, 5)
  ratio H     the pair is in simple sky/natal ratio in the horse chart
  ratio J     the same in the jockey chart
  set         horse : jockey : sky pair set on the pair
  h:j         horse:jockey ratio on the pair
  family      pair-family ratio on the pair (sky-to-natal against natal or sky pair)
  same body   a same-body set on A or on B
  sky         A and B together in the sky (great circle < 5, or a close sky pair)
PAIR FOCUS = the number of different kinds (not items). Counted from tight root items; near items listed beside, not counted.
Derived items are shown under their roots, never counted.
