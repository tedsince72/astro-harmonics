## The reading view – readrace.py v1.0 (md5 3e190be9…, ledger/)
Built straight from the positions (engine sky at the off ±2 and ±30 min; natal noon positions and the hourly birth-day positions), engine family scoring unchanged. Fast points set aside; the Moon KEPT in the sky (it is often the busiest body) and in natal charts, where an item on the natal Moon only counts if it scores at every hour of the birth day.
- Sky: every 80+ pair ranked; busiest body with the Moon kept AND without it (BUSY = either); the clean number each top distance sits on (e.g. 160/9, 13/9, 4φ, 27√2); Applying / Separating at the off; the score half an hour before → after.
- Held how, per sky pair, per chart (the sheets' words): N natal pair (any family), X sky→natal and S self (sky pair's family), item ≥ 40; Type 1 = two or more; LANDS = both sky bodies on one natal body in the sky family+coordinate ≥ 60; LINK = touched on both charts; INSIDE = Type 1 or natal pair; ALONE = no other runner holds it inside.
- Star rule (new, from the raw-distance work): a natal star sits where the sky star is, so "sky planet → natal star" copies the sky pair for every runner [copy] and "sky star → natal planet" repeats the natal pair [=N]; shown, not counted.
- Every item shows raw degrees, distance from its family target, Applying/Separating, and c/p (certain over the birth day or not) on fast natal bodies.
- Receivers (natal bodies taking 2+ landings), repeats of the key sky numbers in the charts (!! = within 0.0003°), rest of the field, and the take-stock checklist.
