RESULT lines in the scored grids read cloth / finish / SP.

METHOD (Eddie, 2 Oct 18:30): "The further away you get from the raw data the further you are from the truth ... read the raw data or as close as you can get without many filters." From T33 each race is read from the detail sheet itself (race sky in full minus the five set-aside points; every runner's links, Type 1s, natal pairs, selves, landings and layers, SHARED items included). The grid is an index only; where the sheet tells a different story from the grid, it is said.
