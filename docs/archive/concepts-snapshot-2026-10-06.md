## The basic idea (grown, not replaced)
- Every horse and every jockey carries a fixed IMPRINT – the distances between its natal bodies at birth.
- The sky at the race carries the ENERGY of the moment.
- NEW: we no longer read single distances only. We read CHORDS – three points whose three distances are all in musical interval ratios. A chord is a note the sky is playing; a natal chart can be TUNED to the same note.
- A winner shows where imprint and moment meet – on one body, at the race time – and now we can also see HOW it is played.

## The language
```
INTERVALS (pure ratios, within 0.15%)
  PERFECT     1  2  3/2  4/3  3  4
  IMPERFECT   5/4  6/5  5/3  8/5  5/2  8/3  9/4
  DISSONANT   √2 (the tritone – "discord in musical harmony")  9/8  16/9  9/5  15/8  16/15
  SEPTIMAL    7/4  7/3  7/2  7/5  7/6  8/7
  HARMONIC    5 6 7 8 9      HIGH  11/…, 13/…      PHI  φ, φ², φ³ …

CHORD = three points, three distances, all in interval ratios
  e.g. 1:2:3   3:5:8 (Fibonacci)   5:8:13   4:5:9   the √2 division 1 : √2 : 1+√2

MEASURES  RA · Dec · Flat Dist · Sky Dist
```
The stars are the instrument: a pair of stars gives a fixed BASE length, and a body sitting in a chord on that base is playing a note against the stars.

## The method – one race at a time, layer by layer
```
STEP 1  IMPRINT (the earlier work)        which steady natal body carries the race's
                                          numbers – in horse, jockey, between them
STEP 2  TUNED-IN LAYERS                   Layer 1 star bases → Nodes → L2 slow → L3 medium
                                          → L4 Sun/Mercury/Venus/Moon → L5 fast points (side note)
STEP 3  MEETING POINTS                    where a tuned chord lands on a Step-1 body
STEP 4  THE 'AGAINST' CHECK               every runner – discord, same note, in/out
Window: 30 min before the off to 15 min after; the minutes around the off matter most.
```

## TUNED IN – the key idea of this phase
Eddie: "This should be it – transit and natal tuned in."
The match is not a sky body hitting a natal point. It is the SKY'S CHORD and the CHART'S CHORD sitting on the SAME BASE.
```
            BASE: Betelgeuse – Capella (Dec 38.592)
                 │                      │
   SKY:   Rahu makes 2:5:7 on it         CHART: Wotever Next's natal Ketu
          (exact 13:32)                         makes 3:4:7 on it (0.001%)

   → the sky and the horse are tuned to the same string
```
Stronger forms:
- UNISON – same base and the same chord type.
- FULL TRIANGLE – the same three points make the same chord in the chart and in the sky:
```
   Joanna Mason natal   Mercury – Pluto – Rahu   RA 2:5:7   (0.001%)
   Sky, 85 s before the off   Mercury – Pluto – Rahu   RA 2:5:7
   → her own chord comes round in the sky (Catterick; nobody else near the race)
```

## The 'against' check – what goes against the others
Eddie: "in a 4 runner race maybe the other runners are negatively affected".
```
A  DISCORD    a runner's chart tuned to a √2 chord in the sky, live in the race window
B  SAME NOTE  two runners in unison with one sky chord – who holds it tighter
C  IN / OUT   tuned chords coming in or going out during the race
```
What we see:
- The winning horse is not caught by live √2 discord in either race.
- Discord falls on beaten runners – Catterick 3rd and 5th; Ffos Las the favourite's jockey (Jack Tudor 5 of 11, including the Moon's √2 exact 20 s before the off and held until 7 s before the finish).
- The only live same-note contest in each race goes to the winning jockey.
- Being IN UNISON with a √2 (your own natal √2 = the sky's √2) looks different from being caught on someone else's √2 – kept apart.
- In/out does not separate.

## What does NOT decide it on its own
- Counts of chords, tuned pairs, unisons (every chart has 100–200 tuned pairs).
- Chord counts rising and falling (the Moon's count, bodies stepping up) – background only.
- The fast points (L5) – about 5–8 chords a second; colour only.
- Exact minutes of slow-body chords – arithmetic; those chords hold for days.
- Shared chords – three or four charts tuned to the same sky chord say little.
