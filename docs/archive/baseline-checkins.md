# Baseline check-ins

A record of what the race-by-race readings show. It lists facts only, with counter-examples alongside, and none of it is a rule. The race reports themselves are in `claude/baseline-race-reports.md`.

**Important:** these races belong to the 48 baseline races, and every reading was made with the result known. Nothing here is confirmed until it is written as an exact rule and tested blind on the 48 test races.

### 8. Candidate rules to write exactly and test blind (nothing is fixed yet)

Written from the above so they can be computed on every partnership before the result:

- **C1 — Outsider thread.** A partnership where one sky body (or pair) is carried on **both** charts through items no other partnership has, from at least two sky pairs sharing that body, or one pair as a Type 1 plus a link. At least one element must be certain on each side, and the pair must not be a birth-period pair (a natal pair on three or more horses in the race).
- **C2 — Favourite race.** No partnership meets C1, and the favourite's own material is concentrated on one chart.
- **C3 — Lay.** The favourite's exclusive links are all "possible", or its material is spread (the most Type 1s or links in the field with no single thread).

**Known problem:** C1 as written would also fire on Swiss Pride / Buick (race 3) and Van Zant / Sullivan (race 7), both in favourite races. So either race type has to be read first (the open D17 question), or C1 needs a sharper form.
