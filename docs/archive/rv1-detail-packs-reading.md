# RV-1 detail packs — reading notes (baseline half, 48 races)

Read with Eddie on 29 Sep 2026. Source: baseline_review v1.1 (500 shuffles, seed 1), detail_packs v1.0 (items with chance_share < 0.05). Say what we see; nothing below is a rule yet. Everything here was found by looking at the baseline, so it has to be confirmed on the test half.

## Pack by pack

**01 / 02 — horse, favourite races, S6-TN unique (not in field, non-DC).** Losers have it and the winner does not in 19/24 races (winner mean 0.25, loser mean 0.82, chance_share 0.0). 01 (certain) and 02 (whole) are identical: an S6-TN entry needs a noon score ≥ 90, so it never drops to 0 in the birth day. Treat 01/02 as one finding. The five winners that do carry an entry: Brazen Akoya (Mercury–Aldebaran), Soldier Of Destiny (Mars–Algorab Dec), Hold Onto The Line (Vertex–Vega), Look Out Louis (Mars–Polaris), Tahani (Sun–Polaris, Jupiter–Polaris). Polaris appears in 3 of the 6 winner entries. A zero count does not pick out the winner: the runner-up also has 0 in 14/24.

**03 — jockey, outsider races, NN Neptune→Sirius RA Ninths.** Winners 10/24 vs losers 8/104 (chance_share 0.022). Both bodies are slow, so this is a birth-period marker: the distances fall in three bands around the Ninths centres 155.56, 166.67 and 177.78. Scores range from 11 to 99; the highest (Buick 99.4) is on a loser. The 10 wins come from 8 jockeys (Mulrennan 2, Morris 2).

**04 — horse, favourite races, TN Transpluto→Mercury Dec Sqrt2 (whole).** Winners 1/24 vs losers 45/139 (0.022). Transpluto hardly moves (Militia 25.6192 in May vs 25.6083 in June), so this is really about natal Mercury's declination. Almost every holder is "possible". Holders are often placed: 10/23 runners-up hold it. The only winner holding it is Tahani, again the exception.

**05 — horse, outsider races, S6-NN unique count.** The winner has the top count on its own in 11/24 races (about 4.6 expected by luck; chance_share 0.022). The margin is usually 1–2; clear leads only at Musselburgh 09/05 (27 v 20) and Bath 03/06 (23 v 15). In favourite races the winner is top on its own only 3/24, about chance level. The losing favourite has the lowest count in 8/24 outsider races. Mister Coffey and Fifty Ball (Newbury 26/11) are both 0: both were foaled 30 Apr 2015 (confirmed by Eddie), so their natal charts are identical to the day. That race is a natural control: identical horse charts, different results (3rd with Bowen, 4th with Moore). Open question: is part of this count really a birth-year difference from the rest of the field?

**06 — partnership, outsider races, Alignment D pair Ketu/Pleiades.** Winning partnership 6/24 vs losing 5/104 (0.03). The sky pair is only active from 11/12/2021 to 16/06/2022 (8 races); when it is present, the winning partnership has it in 6 of the 8. Many of the links are very faint (Sir Chauvelin 0.27 / 0.0; Society Red 0.1 / 0.0). Mulrennan rides 2 of the 6 winners (Newcastle); Tudhope carries the same link twice and loses both times. Several races show more than one holder because D exclusivity is applied per pair/family/coordinate, not per pair.

**07 — horse, outsider races, TN Eris→Pallas Dec Ninths (whole).** Winners 0/24 vs losers 30/104 (0.04). Eris is effectively fixed, so this is natal Pallas's declination. Two winners do hold it, but only in races set aside as date constants (Golden Flame, Heart Throb). 12/22 runners-up hold it. 8/22 losing favourites hold it.

**08 — partnership, outsider races, Alignment A (shared links).** The winner has the lowest count on its own in 9/24, and is in the bottom two in 16/24 (0.042). A is near-saturated (about 275–375 links per partnership) and the differences are 1–3%. Counts vary more between races than within one.

**09 — jockey, outsider races, NN Sun→Venus Dec Whole Number (whole).** Winners 0/24 vs losers 30/104 (0.048). The Sun–Venus declination gap passes a whole degree during the birth day. Luke Morris holds it in two date-constant races he won (a faint link, max 7.2). The 30 loser holdings come from about 20 jockeys (Tudhope 4). 8/21 losing favourites' jockeys hold it.

## Themes

1. **Exclusive vs shared.** What only one runner has goes with winning outsiders (05, 06). Shared, or a lot of it, goes with losing (08). Winning favourites are quiet (01/02). This matches Eddie's earlier finding on the power of exclusive contacts.
2. **Static transit body → natal declination, "possible" only.** Marks "didn't win", often second (04, 07; 09 is the natal-to-natal version).
3. **Birth-period markers.** Several contacts really mark when the horse or jockey was born (03, 04, 06, 07, 09). This is why the same jockeys recur (Mulrennan, Morris, Tudhope).
4. **Date-constant exclusion hides winners** in 07 and 09. The rule needs looking at.

Cautions:
- Races were selected on the result, so these are not real strike rates.
- Field size differs (outsider 5.3 runners, favourite 6.8), and unique counts rise in small fields.
- The combined rule was found by looking.

Candidate D18 runner calls for the test half: (a) favourite with ≥ 1 unique certain TN; (b) (a) plus lowest unique NN.

## Race-type (D17) check so far

- Field-wide averages per runner match between race types once field size is matched (5–6 runners).
- A standout horse (unique NN gap ≥ 2) appears equally often (16 v 16). In outsider races the standout is the winner; in favourite races it is a loser.
- Race-sky pairs at 80+: 73.7 outsider vs 76.5 favourite, with wide overlap.

Not yet tested: fast-pair count (Asc/MC/Vertex/PoF) and the share of runners with unique fixed-star values. Both came from the earlier Selangor work and are race-level candidates.
