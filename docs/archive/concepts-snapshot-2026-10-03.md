# How it works – concept snapshot, 3 Oct 2026 (after 7 upset races)

Written down at Eddie's request (3 Oct 11:52–11:55): "it really helps me to have these concepts written down at certain moments". From the blank-sheet reads T33, T32, R21, R29, R9, R30, R12 (details in blank-sheet-reads.md). Seen with the results known – a working picture, not a rule.

## The basic idea
Every horse and every jockey carries a fixed IMPRINT: the distances between its natal bodies at birth. They never change.
The sky at the off carries the ENERGY of the moment: the distances between the bodies now. A few of them stand out as the race's KEY NUMBERS – the strongest sky pairs, and above all a number the sky plays twice.

The question:
> Which runner's own imprint already holds the sky's key number – on one body – and is the sky reaching that same body at the race time?

## Step 1 – the race's key number
The sky often says its number more than once:

```
T33:  #1 Chiron/Moon   17.777   ┐
      #5 Pallas/Pluto  17.780   ┘  the same number (160/9) twice

R9:   #3 Ceres/Rahu      4.444  ┐
      #10 Sun/Transpluto 44.447 ┘  the same digits, decimal moved
```
When the sky repeats itself, that is the note being played loudest.

## Step 2 – the runner whose imprint already holds it
Look inside each runner's own charts – horse natal, jockey natal, and horse × jockey – for a distance equal to the key number.

```
RACE SKY (the moment)            WOTEVER NEXT (the imprint)

#5 Pallas/Pluto 17.780  ──────►  horse  Betelgeuse–ORCUS  17.782
#4 Betelgeuse/Makemake  ──────►  jockey Arcturus–ORCUS    15.558
            15.554                      (the same number, 140/9)

        → horse AND jockey hold the sky's numbers on the SAME body: Orcus
```
It must sit on a body that does not move over the birth day. A "match" on the Moon, Sun, Mercury or Venus may not exist at the real birth time – the beaten favourites' matches were almost always on those moving bodies.

## Step 3 – the sky touches that same body at the off
Holding the number is not enough: at the race time the sky must reach that body – a top pair lands on it, the busiest body in the sky lands on it, or a contact on it peaks exactly at the off.

```
                    NATAL JUNO (Forget You Not, R12)
                           ▲
       ┌───────────────────┼─────────────────────┐
       │                   │                     │
 #10 Regulus/Transpluto  horse natal          jockey natal
 (φ = 1.618) LANDS on    Juno–Regulus         Ceres–Juno
 Juno, both legs         1.619 (same number)  16.187 (same number x10)
 applying (the moment)   (the imprint)        (the imprint)

 + sky Mars → the jockey's Juno, peaking exactly at the race time
```
The CLOSED LOOP: imprint and moment meet on the same pair and the same body.

## Winners vs beaten favourites
```
WINNER                               BEATEN FAVOURITE
──────                               ────────────────
holds the sky's key number           holds no key number,
on a body that does not move         or only on a moving body

that body is reached by the sky      big strong hubs – but the sky
at the off (busiest body, a top      does not touch them
pair, or exact at the off)

often horse AND jockey meet          strength in general
on the same body                     (counts, royal stars)
```

## What does NOT decide it
How MUCH a runner has – big counts, many strong pairs, lots of landings, royal stars, general hubs, half numbers, midpoints. A runner can be very "strong" and still lose if the sky is not playing its note.

## In one sentence
> The winner is the runner whose own fixed imprint already holds the number the sky is repeating that day, on one steady body – and the sky reaches that body at the race time.

## Eddie's guiding point (3 Oct 11:55)
"The deeper we go in more examples the clearer it will become, because these outsiders must all have something standout in transit and natal at that moment – even if it was a negative on all the other runners that left them the last standing."
So each read also looks for what each beaten runner LACKS or what goes against it (negatives), not only what the winner has.
